## The project

Official platform for Hult Prize at UNI, the university edition of the international social entrepreneurship competition. It brings together the competition landing page, participant team registration and the judges' scoring system.

## My role

As Head of IT since August 2026:

- I lead the team that provides technology support for the competition.
- I direct the rollout of the official landing page, the team registration system and the scoring system.
- I organize the work in sprints with GitHub Projects and set GitFlow as the branching and review standard.

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) and React with Tailwind CSS and shadcn/ui on the frontend; Supabase (PostgreSQL, Auth and Storage) with Prisma ORM for data; deployed on Vercel.
