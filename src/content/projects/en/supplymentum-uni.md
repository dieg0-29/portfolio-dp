## The project

End-to-end web development to give visibility to the Supplymentum UNI student organization: an institutional landing page that manages information about its areas and centralizes media resources. Built between July and August 2026.

## My contribution

- I built the content management system (CMS), an admin area to dynamically update the board members, the organization's areas and the site's content without touching the code.

## Stack

NestJS and Supabase for the backend and data; JavaScript, Tailwind CSS, HTML and CSS on the frontend.
