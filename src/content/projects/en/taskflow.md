## The problem

Small teams were using spreadsheets and chat threads to coordinate work, which led to duplicated information and little visibility into progress.

## The solution

A web app with collaborative Kanban boards where every change syncs in real time across all members.

- Drag and drop cards between columns with instant updates.
- User roles (admin, editor, viewer) and private boards.
- Notifications when you're assigned or mentioned on a task.

## What I learned

Designing a data model that supports concurrent changes, and optimizing long-list rendering in React.
